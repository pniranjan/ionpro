AbhiYahan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

AbhiYahan is an app for finding places to go and things to do near you. This policy explains what the AbhiYahan app for Android ("the app") does with information about you. In short: there are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your location is used only to show you what is nearby.

Location

  • With your permission, the app reads your phone's location to find places and events near you and to show how far away they are.
  • To do this, the app sends your coordinates to our server with each request for nearby places and events. The server uses them to sort the results and then discards them. We do not save your location, build a history of where you have been, or link it to you.
  • You can say no, or turn off location for the app at any time in your phone's settings. The app then shows what is near the city centre instead.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No contacts, photos, files, messages or call logs.
  • No advertising identifiers, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.

Information kept on your phone

The app remembers your filter choices (for example, the categories and distance you picked) on your phone so they are still set next time. This stays on your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Our service providers

The app's content (places, events and photos) is served by Supabase, our hosting provider. Like any web service, their servers keep short-lived technical logs of requests, such as IP address and time, for security and to keep the service running. We do not use these logs to identify or profile anyone.

When you tap Navigate, the app opens Google Maps (or your browser) with the destination. What happens there is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or share information about you with anyone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
